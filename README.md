TruthGuard — AI Fraud & Misinformation Detector

TruthGuard is an AI-powered detection and verification platform designed to help users identify fake news, job scams, phishing attempts, financial fraud, and medical misinformation.

The platform combines Google Gemini, Firebase, Google Safe Browsing, and machine-learning techniques to analyze suspicious content and provide understandable risk assessments.

🚀 Key Features
📰 Fake News Detection

Analyze news articles, claims, and other content to identify potentially misleading information.

Classification:

🟢 Real / Likely Reliable
🟡 Suspicious
🔴 Likely Fake / Misleading

Supports:

Text
Images
Video content
Job Scam Detection

Analyze job advertisements and recruitment messages to identify common scam indicators such as:

Unrealistic salaries
Upfront payment requests
Fake recruitment claims
Suspicious contact information
Urgency and pressure tactics
Requests for sensitive information
Phishing & Malicious URL Detection

Analyze URLs and identify potentially dangerous websites using URL reputation and security signals.

The system can help detect:

Phishing websites
Suspicious domains
Malicious URLs
Fake login pages
Impersonation websites
Financial Fraud Detection

Identify suspicious financial messages and potential fraud attempts, including:

Fake payment requests
Banking scams
Investment scams
Lottery/prize scams
UPI/payment fraud
Impersonation scams
Medical Misinformation Detection

Analyze health-related claims and identify potentially misleading or unsupported medical information.

Disclaimer: TruthGuard is an informational tool and does not replace professional medical advice.

AI-Generated Media Analysis:

Analyze images and supported media content for potential signs of AI generation or manipulation.

Detection results are probabilistic and should not be treated as definitive proof of manipulation.
